## 1. Press release (English) — for 80.lv, CG Channel, CGSociety, Blender Nation, Toolfarm news, newsletters

**Layerbase Luxury Vision releases Layerbase Sequence Player, a free image sequence player for 3D and VFX artists**

*Opens EXR renders in under half a second, with ACES 2.0, AgX and Cryptomatte, and exports ProRes and H.264 movies. Free for commercial use.*

**Italy, [DATE]** — Layerbase Luxury Vision, a studio specialized in luxury product visualization, today released
Layerbase Sequence Player 1.2 for Windows. The free, open-source tool is designed for artists who need to review renders quickly,
see them with the correct color management and turn them into client-ready movies without opening a compositing application.

Layerbase Sequence Player opens a whole image sequence from a double-click on any frame. In internal tests on 1080p EXR
renders, the first frame is on screen in about 0.3–0.4 seconds, while upcoming frames are decoded in parallel into a RAM cache.

**Key features**

- **Formats:** EXR (including multi-layer and multi-part), DPX, TIFF, PNG, JPEG, TGA, BMP, HDR and PSD.
- **Color management on the GPU with OpenColorIO 2.5:** ACES 2.0 and ACES 1.3 built in, custom config.ocio files,
  automatic detection of installed Blender configs, and built-in AgX, AgX Punchy and AgX Golden views.
- **Multi-layer EXR:** switch between render passes with one click, for viewing or export.
- **Cryptomatte:** view IDs, highlight or isolate objects and materials with a click, and export the result with the mask
  as a ProRes 4444 alpha channel. Cryptomatte stored in a separate sequence, as Octane renders it, can be loaded on top
  of the beauty pass and matched by frame number.
- **Movie export:** H.264 and H.265 (including NVIDIA NVENC) and ProRes 422 Proxy/LT/422/HQ/4444, with the colors
  exactly as shown in the viewer and correct color tags in the file. FFmpeg is included.
- **Batch conversion:** convert every sequence in a folder and its subfolders with the same settings.
- **Windows integration:** appears in "Open with" and Default apps; a portable version runs without installation.

"We built it for our own daily work on product renders: we wanted to check a sequence in ACES or AgX, isolate the piece
with Cryptomatte and send a preview to the client in a couple of minutes," said [NAME], [ROLE] at Layerbase Luxury Vision.
"It became useful enough that we decided to give it to the community for free."

**Availability**

Layerbase Sequence Player 1.2 is available now for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) as an installer and as a portable zip.
It is free and open source under the MIT license, also for commercial use, with an English and Italian interface.Source code: https://github.com/polloviz/Layerbase-Sequence-Player
